🎯 Night-before top 10

  • Shot window 17–27 sec, perfect = 22 • short = finer, long = coarser
  • Order: Flavor → Shots → Milk
  • Regular temp 160–170°, kids 110–120°, burnt over 180°
  • Default cream = Breve • cream scoops 1-2-2
  • Breve originals (1-2-2): Blondie, Smooth 7, Cinnamon Roll, Sweet & Salty, Cookie Butter • Mocha originals (1-1-2): Banana Bread, Brunette
  • Ice fill lines 8-12-16 (kids 5) • hot 8-10-12
  • Dub = 2, Quad = 4, Triple 7 = 6 shots any size
  • Light ice = +4 oz (matcha: +1 scoop matcha)
  • Special 7 = decaf, 1 shot only, milk alt, or sugar-free
  • Big 3: Genuine Interactions, Open Body Language, Customer Interactions
